# Robinson Vidva

**Computational Biologist** · More than 16 years in systems biology modeling, multi-omics analysis, and drug discovery · Washington, DC area

Twelve years building and leading precision-oncology and immunology modeling platforms at Cellworks, three years running an independent consultancy, and, since 2025, bioinformatics and biostatistics for a neonatal neuroscience laboratory at Children's National Hospital (Kratimenos Lab). I integrate mutational, gene-expression, imaging, and clinical data to model treatment response and identify biomarkers. Full CV, publications, and patents at [robinsonvidva.com](https://robinsonvidva.com).

### Tools

Free, open-source, and most run entirely in the browser.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|

| [enrichlite](https://robinsonvidva.com/enrichlite/) · [code](https://github.com/robinson-vidva/enrichlite) | Gene-set over-representation analysis in the browser (GO, Reactome, Hallmark) |
